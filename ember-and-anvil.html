How we roast

Four steps, no shortcuts

  • 01

    Source direct

    We buy from three importers who visit the farms themselves, every season.
  • 02

    Cup before we buy

    Every lot is cupped blind against the last three years of that farm's harvest.
  • 03

    Roast in small drums

    12kg batches on a 1955 drum roaster, tracked and logged by hand.
  • 04

    Rest, then ship

    Beans rest 5–10 days post-roast before bags go out the door.
